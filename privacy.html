✦ AskLightly — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

AskLightly (“the app”, “we”) is a Windows utility that lets you act on text you select — rewriting, fixing, explaining, summarizing, or answering questions about it — using a third‑party AI service of your choice. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your data.

The short version
  • AskLightly only processes text you deliberately select. It never scans your screen or reads pages on its own.
  • When you use a feature, the text you selected is sent to the AI provider you chose (Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, DeepSeek, or xAI) to generate a result.
  • We operate no server. Your text goes directly from your computer to that provider — never to us or any middle‑man.
  • We collect no analytics, telemetry, or personal information.
  • Your API key and settings are stored only on your own computer.

What information the app handles

Selected text

When you invoke an AskLightly action, the text you have highlighted (and, for the “Ask Anything” feature, the instruction you type) is sent to the AI provider you selected in order to produce a response. This is required for the app to function. AskLightly does not store a history of your selections or results.

Your API key

To connect to an AI provider you supply your own API key. It is stored locally in the app’s settings file on your computer. You may optionally enable encryption, which secures the key using Windows account‑based protection so it can only be read by your Windows user on that machine. Your API key is sent only to the corresponding AI provider to authenticate your own requests.

App settings

Your preferences (chosen provider, model, theme, shortcut, custom options, etc.) are stored locally on your computer. They are not transmitted anywhere.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or account information.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or telemetry.
  • We do not send your text, keys, or settings to any server operated by us.
  • We do not sell or share any data — because we do not receive any.

Third‑party AI providers

The actual AI processing is performed by the provider you choose. When you use the app, your selected text is handled by that provider under their terms and privacy policies:

  • Anthropic (Claude): anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Google (Gemini): policies.google.com/privacy
  • OpenAI: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • DeepSeek: deepseek.com privacy policy
  • xAI / SpaceXAI (Grok): x.ai/legal/privacy-policy

Note that DeepSeek is operated by a company based in China, and your selected text is processed there under that provider's policies. Choose the provider that suits the sensitivity of your work.

Please review the privacy policy of the provider you use. If you handle sensitive or confidential information, be aware it will be processed by that provider.

Where your data lives

Everything AskLightly stores — your API key and settings — stays in your local Windows user profile on your own device. Removing the app or deleting its settings file removes this data. There is no cloud account to delete because none is created.

Children’s privacy

AskLightly is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will be revised. Material changes will be reflected in the app’s store listing.

Contact
